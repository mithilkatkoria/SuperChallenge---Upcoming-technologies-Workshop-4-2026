# Inspire Scholars Workshop 4 (2026) | Quantum Computing

> An interactive research poster I created for **Inspire Scholars Workshop 4: Upcoming Technologies**, exploring how quantum computing differs from classical computing and why it matters.

**Live project:** https://super-challenge-upcoming-technologi.vercel.app/

## Project

For Workshop 4, I chose **quantum computing** as my upcoming technology and built the final research piece as a responsive web experience rather than a static document.

The page explains the topic visually, moving from the basic idea of bits and qubits into the core concepts behind quantum computation, possible applications, current limitations and my own reflection on the technology.

### What the poster covers

- **Classical bits vs qubits** and why quantum information behaves differently
- **Superposition**, **entanglement** and **interference**
- A simplified step-by-step view of how a quantum computation works
- Potential uses in **drug discovery**, **cryptography**, **AI / machine learning** and **optimisation**
- Present-day challenges including **decoherence**, **error rates** and **scaling**
- A short personal reflection on why quantum computing is important to follow

## Why I built it as a website

The aim was not only to research an emerging technology, but to communicate it clearly.

I used the web format so I could combine:

- responsive layouts
- animated visual elements
- a custom quantum / orbital motif
- structured information cards
- progressive sections that make a difficult topic easier to scan

The result is a single-page research poster that works on desktop and mobile and can be shared as a live link.
